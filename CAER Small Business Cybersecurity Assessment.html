You Can't Protect What You Don't Know Is There.

Most small businesses we meet have an ISP-provided router, a handful of Windows or Mac computers, and a flat network where everything can talk to everything else. Along the way, printers, cameras, a NAS, old wireless equipment and other devices get added — often with no one tracking what's connected or whether it's secure.

CAER temporarily connects a security assessment system to your network to identify devices, services, and vulnerabilities. We don't test for weaknesses by trying to break in — we look for what's exposed and explain it in plain English.

Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Will the assessment disrupt my business?+

No. It's designed to run in the background without interrupting your normal workday. We schedule it for a time that works for you and your team.

Do you need my passwords?+

Not for the Essential or Complete assessments. Any step that would require elevated access — like an authenticated scan in the Comprehensive Assessment — is explained up front and only done with your authorization.

Do you install software on my computers?+

The assessment works primarily through a temporary device connected to your network, not permanent software installed on every computer. Anything beyond that is explained before it happens.

What happens after the assessment?+

You receive an executive-friendly report and a review meeting where we walk through what we found, what matters most, and what we recommend. What you do with that report is entirely up to you.

How long does the assessment take?+

Most Essential and Complete assessments are completed within a few business days from connection to report. Comprehensive assessments vary with network size and complexity.

Do I have to become a managed IT client?+

No. There is no obligation to hire CAER for remediation or managed services. You get the findings and recommendations whether you fix them yourself, use another provider, or have CAER handle the work.

Can you fix the problems you find?+

Yes, if you'd like us to — but it's never required. The assessment is a complete, standalone deliverable on its own.

What if I only have one or two computers?+

The Essential Assessment is built for businesses this size — accountants, solo attorneys, small clinics and similar offices. You still have a router, a network, cloud accounts and devices worth checking.

Can you review Microsoft 365 security?+

Yes. The Complete and Comprehensive Assessments include a Microsoft 365 security review covering MFA, administrative accounts, and general email and cloud security configuration.

Is this a penetration test?+

No. This is a cybersecurity and vulnerability assessment, not a penetration test. CAER identifies vulnerabilities and security weaknesses but does not attempt to exploit systems, unless separately contracted and specifically authorized for that purpose.
