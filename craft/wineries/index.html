The Reality of Running a Winery

IT problems don't take a day off — especially weekends

Tasting rooms run on weekends, holidays, and harvest deadlines. Generic office IT support wasn't built around that schedule — CAER Technologies was.

  • POS goes down mid-pour A frozen terminal or dropped card reader during a busy tasting weekend costs sales — and first impressions.
  • Wine club & guest data is a target Card numbers, club member details, and guest lists are exactly what attackers look for at hospitality businesses.
  • One dead WiFi zone ruins the visit Tasting room, patio, and barrel room all need coverage — not just the front desk router.
  • Harvest season doesn't wait for IT Crush pad scales, inventory systems, and seasonal staff logins all need to work the moment fruit comes in.
