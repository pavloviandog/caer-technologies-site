The Reality of Running a Meadery

IT problems don't take a day off — especially on release weekends

Tasting rooms run on weekends and small-batch production runs on its own clock. Generic office IT support wasn't built around either — CAER Technologies was.

  • POS goes down mid-pour A frozen terminal or dropped card reader during a busy tasting weekend costs sales — and first impressions.
  • Mead club & guest data is a target Card numbers, mead club member details, and guest lists are exactly what attackers look for at hospitality businesses.
  • One dead WiFi zone ruins the visit Tasting room, patio, and event space all need coverage — not just the front desk router.
  • Small-batch production doesn't wait for IT Fermentation records, honey sourcing logs, and batch tracking all need to work without interruption.
