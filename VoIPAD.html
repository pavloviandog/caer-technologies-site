FAQ

Common Questions About Business VoIP

Can I keep my current phone number?
Yes. We can port your existing numbers to the new system in most cases. We handle the entire porting process so there's no disruption to your business.
How long does setup take?
Most systems are up and running within a few days. The exact timeline depends on your team size and number transfer timing — we'll give you a clear estimate upfront.
Do I need to buy new phones?
Not always. We can often use compatible existing hardware. If new desk phones are needed, we'll recommend the right options and include them in your quote.
What happens if the internet goes down?
Calls can be automatically routed to mobile devices or backup numbers so you never lose contact with customers — even during an outage.
Can staff use their personal cell phones for business calls?
Yes. Your team can make and receive business calls from their smartphones while keeping their personal numbers completely private. Great for remote and field staff.
Is VoIP as reliable as a traditional phone system?
When properly configured on a solid network, VoIP is highly reliable for business use. We assess your network as part of the setup to ensure your system performs the way it should.
