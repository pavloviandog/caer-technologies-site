Every Fiscal Year, Certified Training Is Due

Texas Government Code §§2054.5191 and 2054.5193 require certain state and local government employees and officials to complete a certified AI awareness program and a certified cybersecurity program, then report completions to the state. The cybersecurity requirement also reaches contractors with access to a Texas computer system or database. A standard, non-certified course does not satisfy the requirement — the Texas Department of Information Resources (DIR), in consultation with the Public Sector AI Systems Advisory Board, certifies which programs count.
