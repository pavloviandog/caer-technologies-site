A TAKEAWAY FROM US

Five ground rules for using AI at work

Share these with your team this week. If any of them feel hard to put in place, that's exactly what our consultation is for.

  1. 1
    Keep sensitive data out of public AI toolsNo customer records, health or financial details, passwords, or confidential documents.
  2. 2
    Use business accounts, not personal onesBusiness plans give you more control over how your data is stored and used.
  3. 3
    Turn on multi-factor authenticationProtect every AI and Microsoft 365 account with MFA.
  4. 4
    Check the output before you send itAI can sound confident and still be wrong. A person reviews anything that goes to a customer.
  5. 5
    Write it downA one-page AI use policy tells your team which tools are approved and what's off-limits.
