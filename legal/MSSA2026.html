This Managed Security Services Agreement ("MSSA") is made and entered into on the Effective Date by and between the Client and CAER Technologies, LLC, a limited liability company existing under the laws of Texas ("Service Provider"). Client and Service Provider are each individually referred to as a "Party" and collectively as the "Parties."

Client and Service Provider desire for Service Provider to provide certain Services as defined herein and further described in Appendix A and B; in consideration of the mutual promises set forth herein and other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and adequacy of which are hereby acknowledged, the Parties agree as follows:

1. Definitions

The capitalized terms used in this MSSA have the meanings set forth below, or if not defined below, the meanings set forth elsewhere in this MSSA.

  • "Administrative Data" – Data concerning registration, use, and administration of Third-Party Products, including telemetry and access logs. Does not include Client Content itself.
  • "Applicable Law" – All laws, statutes, ordinances, regulations, rules, orders, and decrees that apply to or govern the performance of a Party's rights or obligations under this MSSA.
  • "Client Systems, Content, and Materials" – All sites, information systems, databases, servers, software, applications, licensed third-party systems granted to Service Provider, and all documents, data, and materials necessary for Service Provider to perform the Services.
  • "Confidential Information" – All information of any nature and in any form furnished by the Disclosing Party, including customer information, financial data, technical know-how, and systems documentation, excluding information (1) already known to the Receiving Party, (2) publicly available without breach, (3) rightfully obtained from a third party without restriction, or (4) independently developed by the Receiving Party.
  • "Client IP" – Any Intellectual Property Rights owned by or licensed to Client which Client makes available to Service Provider to perform Services.
  • "Damages" – All losses, claims, obligations, demands, assessments, penalties, liabilities, damages, costs, and expenses (including reasonable attorneys' and consulting fees).
  • "Deliverables" – Any tangible work product furnished by Service Provider to Client as part of the Services, as set forth in Appendix A.
  • "Disclosing Party" – The Party who furnishes Confidential Information to the other Party.
  • "Dispute" – Any controversy, disagreement, claim, or dispute between the Parties arising out of or relating to this MSSA.
  • "Fees" – Service Provider's compensation for performance of the Services (exclusive of reimbursable expenses or applicable taxes), as set forth in Appendix A and B.
  • "Force Majeure" – Any event or circumstance that adversely affects, prevents, or delays performance of obligations, and is beyond the reasonable control of the affected Party.
  • "Intellectual Property Rights" / "IPR" – All rights in relation to inventions and designs, including patent, copyright, trademarks, trade secrets, and know-how.
  • "Personal Data" – Any information that can, directly or indirectly, identify a natural person as defined by Applicable Law.
  • "Prudent Industry Practices" – Practices generally observed by managed IT- and cybersecurity-services providers as of the Effective Date.
  • "Service Provider IP" – Any IPR owned by or licensed to Service Provider or its subcontractors or Third-Party Suppliers, including all materials, tools, methods, and technologies used or created in performing Services.
  • "Service Provider Materials" – All materials, tools, methods, processes, technology, algorithms, software, data sources, and proprietary information used or created by Service Provider in performance of Services or development of Deliverables.
  • "Receiving Party" – The Party who receives Confidential Information from the Disclosing Party.
  • "Statement of Work" / "SOW" – A document setting forth a description of Services, Deliverables, Fees, and other requirements as mutually executed by the Parties.
  • "Services" – The services Service Provider provides to Client as further described in Appendix A and Appendix B, including associated Deliverables.
  • "Third-Party Products" – Third-party hardware or software products incorporated into the Services or procured by Service Provider on behalf of Client.
  • "Third-Party Services" – Services provided to Client by a Third-Party Supplier, directly or indirectly, as part of the Services.
  • "Third-Party Supplier" – Any third party providing Third-Party Products or Third-Party Services as part of the Services.
  • "Unmanaged Devices" – Any computing devices (laptops, desktops, servers, or mobile devices) not actively enrolled in or protected by the Service Provider's Managed Services, whether due to the Client's decision or use of third-party contractor-owned devices.

2.1 Service Provider will provide the Services described in Appendix A and Appendix B, which are hereby incorporated by reference.

2.2 Client agrees to provide access to and make available all Client Systems, Content, and Materials as Service Provider may need to provide the Services, including all necessary consents, permissions, and authorizations. If the Services require installation and use of hardware and/or software, Client will facilitate installation and provide physical space, electrical power, internet connection, and physical access as communicated by Service Provider. 2.4 Service Provider may subcontract any of its obligations under this MSSA in its sole discretion, provided Service Provider remains liable for performance of its obligations.

  • CoreCAER – Associate, Associate+  (Monitoring & Entry Level)
  • ManageCAER – Manager  (Fully Managed IT)

Each tier defines scope, response, and security controls. Services not explicitly included are out-of-scope and billable. Lower-tier services (CoreCAER) are limited in scope and do not include full management or remediation.

3.1 In consideration of Service Provider's performance of Services, Client will pay Service Provider those Fees and Hourly Rates as provided in Appendix A and Appendix B.

3.2 Invoices will be issued in advance for recurring services and in arrears for additional services or reimbursable expenses.

If Client disputes any portion of an invoice in good faith, Client shall pay the undisputed portion and promptly advise Service Provider in writing of the disputed portion. Client shall not set off any amounts due under this MSSA.

This Appendix defines the minimum security standards, risk expectations, and compliance alignment associated with Services provided by CAER Technologies. 3. Security Tiers & Expectations

3.1 CoreCAER (Associate / Associate+)

Designed for foundational monitoring and visibility only. Advanced EDR/MDR services such as Huntress are not included unless separately added.

Includes: Endpoint protection (basic), Monitoring and alerting, Limited security controls, Basic visibility into system health.

Does NOT include: Active threat response, Compliance alignment, Advanced security controls EDR/MDR, Formal governance controls, Advanced logging or SIEM.

⚠ Risk Level: HIGH — CoreCAER is not suitable for regulated environments or high-risk organizations/environments.
3.2 ManageCAER (Manager)

Designed for business-grade security and IT operational stability.

Includes: Managed endpoint protection, Patch management, Managed entry-level firewall administration, Standard security stack, Backup management, Limited incident response.

Limitations: Limited compliance alignment, No formal governance framework, Advanced reporting may require additional services.

⚠ Risk Level: MODERATE
3.3 SecureCAER (Professional / Executive)

Designed for security-first and compliance-aligned environments.

Includes: Endpoint detection & response (EDR), Managed email security and threat protection, Security monitoring and response, MFA enforcement guidance, Vulnerability scanning, Policy enforcement and hardening, Backup strategy and validation, Compliance-aligned controls (HIPAA/NIST where applicable).

⚠ Risk Level: LOWER (not eliminated)
3.4 SecureCAER Executive

Designed for organizations requiring governance, risk management, compliance alignment, and business continuity planning.

Includes (in addition to Professional): Quarterly security reviews, Executive security reporting, Security policy review assistance, Vendor coordination, Advanced backup validation, Governance-focused planning.

⚠ Risk Level: REDUCED (not eliminated)

4. Minimum Security Standards

The following controls are recommended as baseline security standards:

4.1 Identity & Access
  • Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)
  • Strong password policies
  • Role-based access control
4.2 Endpoint Security
  • Antivirus / EDR protection
  • Disk encryption (where applicable)
  • Device compliance enforcement
4.3 Network Security
  • Managed firewall
  • Secure remote access (VPN or equivalent)
  • Network segmentation (recommended for larger environments)
4.4 Data Protection
  • Regular backups
  • Backup monitoring
  • Periodic restore validation (SecureCAER recommended)
4.5 User Awareness
  • Security awareness training (recommended)
  • Phishing awareness
