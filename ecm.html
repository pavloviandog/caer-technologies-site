Ethan is a Los Angeles-based actor and screenwriter who graduated from AMDA — the American Musical and Dramatic Academy with dual training in Acting and Screenwriting.

His work spans dramatic performance and original narrative writing, drawing on a rigorous conservatory education to deliver compelling characters both in front of the camera and on the page.

He is actively seeking roles and collaborative opportunities in film and television, while developing his slate of original screenplays.
