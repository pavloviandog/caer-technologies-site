The Reality of Running a Brewery

IT problems don't take a day off — especially release day

Taprooms run on weekends, live music nights, and release-day lines out the door. Generic office IT support wasn't built around that schedule — CAER Technologies was.

  • POS goes down mid-pour A frozen terminal during a packed release night costs sales — and turns away the line at the door.
  • Mug club & guest data is a target Card numbers, mug club member details, and guest lists are exactly what attackers look for at hospitality businesses.
  • One dead WiFi zone ruins the visit Taproom, beer garden, and patio all need coverage — not just the bar's router.
  • Brew days don't wait for IT Batch tracking, canning line systems, and inventory all need to work the moment brew day starts.

  • Reliable WiFi, taproom to beer garden Coverage planned for indoor, patio, and outdoor event space — not an afterthought.
  • Data backed up and watched Mug club, accounting, and batch/inventory data protected and recoverable.
